## Goal
Detect people without helmets in monitored areas from multiple cameras (smart, industrial, security), save an event or trigger an alarm, with or without person identification, without duplicate incidents across cameras.

## Known requirements
- Input: RTSP/ONVIF streams; optional camera-side motion/analytics triggers (ONVIF events, webhooks, MQTT).
- Trigger: person-only motion trigger generated by us when we consume raw streams.
- Analysis: helmet / no helmet on person triggers.
- Output: stored event (snapshot, clip with pre-roll, metadata) and/or alarm (MQTT, webhook, relay).
- Dedup: across cameras, no biometric/face ID; use appearance, position, timing.
- Identification: optional and separately switchable.

## Open questions (resolve in interview)
- Number of cameras, resolution, mounting height/angle, indoor/outdoor, night/IR.
- Hardware target: edge (Jetson/Hailo/OpenVINO) vs central GPU.
- Latency requirement for alarms.
- Uniform/hi-vis policy at the site (limits appearance re-ID).
- Legal basis and retention (GDPR, labour law, works council).
- Identification method if enabled (roster, badge, face) and who approves it.

## Out of scope (initial)
- Other PPE classes (vests, goggles), fall detection, access control.

- `python eval/run.py` reports helmet precision >= TBD and recall >= TBD on `eval/clips/`.
- Dedup: incidents-per-real-person <= TBD on the multi-camera eval set.
